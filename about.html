About Us

Clean cars, happy drivers.

Our Story

SparkWash started in 2018 as a small two-bay garage with one simple goal: give every car the same care we'd give our own. Today, our trained team washes and details hundreds of vehicles every week.


We use pH-balanced soaps, soft microfiber towels, and water-saving equipment to keep your car — and the planet — in great shape.

Why Choose Us

Trained Staff

Every team member is trained in safe washing and detailing techniques.

Eco-Friendly

Biodegradable products and water recycling systems.

Fast Service

Most exterior washes are done in under 30 minutes.
