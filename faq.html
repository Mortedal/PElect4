Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your visit.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome for basic and premium washes. We recommend booking ahead for interior detailing and polishing.

How long does a wash take?

A basic wash takes about 20–30 minutes. Full detailing can take 2–3 hours depending on the car's condition.

Is pressure washing safe for my paint?

Yes. We use controlled pressure, the right nozzle distance, and a foam pre-wash to lift dirt without scratching.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, credit/debit cards, and popular mobile wallets.

Do you clean leather interiors?

Absolutely. Our Leather Care service cleans and conditions seats to keep them soft and crack-free.

What if I'm not happy with the result?

Let us know before you leave and we'll re-do the affected area free of charge.
